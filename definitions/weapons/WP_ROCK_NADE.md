# Rock Grenade

`WP_ROCK_NADE`

> Primitive thrown projectile and customizable grenade template.

## What it does

A basic thrown rock dealing small impact and splash damage. Sits in the 4th and final position of the Quick-Throw grenade queue. Commonly used as a template weapon for custom explosives.

## Mechanics & Stats

- **Base Damage & Radius:** 2 damage, 2 unit splash radius.
- **Fire Modes:** Primary fire is an immediate impact detonation; secondary fire is a standard physics bounce.
- **Ammo:** Dedicated ammo pool. Each level of `MB_ATT_ROCK_NADE` grants +1 Rock.
- **Quick-Throw:** Sits 4th in Quick-Throw queue.
