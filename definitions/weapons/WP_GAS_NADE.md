# Dioxis Gas Grenade

`WP_GAS_NADE`

> Chemical warfare canister dispersing caustic dioxis vapor.

## What it does

Fills an enclosed choke point with corrosive chemical gas that inflicts continuous damage over time against organic soldiers and bypasses conventional shields.
