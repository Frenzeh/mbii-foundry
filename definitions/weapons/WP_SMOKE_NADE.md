# Smoke Grenade

`WP_SMOKE_NADE`

> Tactical obscuration canister deploying dense thermal smoke.

## What it does

Disperses a thick smoke screen that blocks line of sight, preventing enemy snipers from targeting corridors and disabling automated turret tracking.
