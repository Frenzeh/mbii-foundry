# Bacta Bomb

`WP_BACTA_BOMB`

> Deployed medical canister dispersing pressurized restorative bacta mist.

## What it does

Creates a localized cloud of aerated bacta that rapidly restores the health of all friendly personnel within the dispersal perimeter.

## Mechanics & Stats

- **Role:** Tactical Support / Area Sustain
- **Effect:** Sustained health restoration for friendly fireteams
