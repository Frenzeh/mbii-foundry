# Tusken Cycler Rifle

`WP_CYCLER`

> Primitive projectile firearm firing solid kinetic slugs.

## What it does

The Cycler rifle is a heavy, single-shot ballistic slugthrower favored by Tusken Raiders. Unlike blaster weapons that produce coherent plasma bolts, the Cycler fires heavy physical projectiles that cannot be deflected by lightsabers and inflict severe blunt kinetic trauma upon impact.

## Mechanics & Stats

- **Role:** Kinetic Marksman / Anti-Saber Sniper
- **Projectile:** Solid ballistic slug (ignores lightsaber deflection)
- **Alt-Fire:** Optical zoom scope targeting
- **Ammunition:** Projectile ammunition pool
