# Golan Arms FA3 Flechette

`WP_FA3_FLECHETTE`

> Heavy anti-infantry flechette shotgun firing canister spreads of razor projectiles.

## What it does

The FA3 Flechette is a combat shotgun variant engineered by Golan Arms for CQB breach suppression. It discharges dense volleys of anti-personnel flechette darts that saturate corridors and chew through light shielding. Deployed primarily on Clone Flechette Specialists and Imperial EVO Troopers.

## Mechanics & Stats

- **Role:** Close-Quarters Breach / Shrapnel Spread
- **Primary Fire:** Multi-pellet flechette spread with heavy point-blank kinetic impulse.
- **Alt-Fire:** Bouncing canister anti-personnel projectile.
