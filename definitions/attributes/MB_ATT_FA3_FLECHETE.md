# FA3 Flechette Mastery

`MB_ATT_FA3_FLECHETE`

> Ranks and ammunition capacity for the Golan Arms FA3 Flechette.

## What it does

Controls pellet density, alternate fire explosive canister capacity, and cycling speed for `WP_FA3_FLECHETTE`.

## Valid Values

- **Rank 1:** Base shotgun issue with standard flechette spread.
- **Rank 2:** Tightened choke with increased reserve ammunition.
- **Rank 3:** Maximum pellet discharge, explosive alt-fire canister capability, and rapid cycling.
