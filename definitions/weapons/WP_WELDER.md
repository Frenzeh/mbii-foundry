# Welder / Fusioncutter

`WP_WELDER`

> Short-range repair tool and cutting torch.

## What it does

Primary fire emits a high-frequency thermal arc that deals rapid continuous damage at point-blank range, igniting targets on fire at Rank 2+. Secondary fire repairs friendly armor and droid health, scaling down against Beskar and SBD heavy plating.

## Mechanics & Stats

- **Role:** Utility / Support / Repair
- **Primary Fire:** Thermal cutting arc (ignites targets at `MB_ATT_WELDER` $\ge 2$).
- **Secondary Fire:** Armor & Droid HP restoration on allies.
- **Armor Resistance Scaling:** Secondary repair is less effective against targets with high levels of Beskar or specialized droid plating.
