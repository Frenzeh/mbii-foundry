# Battle Droid

`MB_CLASS_BATTLEDROID`

> Mechanical infantry with biological immunities and severe ion vulnerability.

## What it does

Represents B1/BX battle droids in the OpenJK engine.

## Built-in Mechanics

- **Immunities:** Completely immune to Poison, Sonic disorientation, Locational headshot multipliers, and Drowning.
- **Vulnerabilities:** Takes **5x damage** from Ion and Pulse weapons unless protected by a Battery resource buffer.
