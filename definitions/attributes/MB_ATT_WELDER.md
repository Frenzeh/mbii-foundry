# Welder / Repair Tool

`MB_ATT_WELDER`

> Ranks and thermal properties for the Welder / Fusioncutter.

## What it does

Controls damage, burn effects, and armor/droid repair efficiency for `WP_WELDER`.

## Valid Values

- **Rank 1:** Base cutting damage; secondary fire repairs ally armor and droid health.
- **Rank 2:** High thermal output; primary fire lights victims on fire.
- **Rank 3:** Maximum cutting speed and highest repair transfer rate.

## Mechanics & Interactions

- **Armor Resistance Scaling:** Ally repair efficiency is reduced against targets with high levels of Beskar or SBD specialty armor.
