# Flash Grenade

`WP_FLASH_NADE`

> Tactical disorientation grenade with concussive blinding burst.

## What it does

Detonates with an intense concussive and photonic flash that completely blinds targets within its effective blast zone, disorienting both infantry and Force users.

## Mechanics & Stats

- **Role:** Tactical Breach / Crowd Disorientation
- **Blast Area:** 200/200 units AoE radius (tuned in R22.4)
- **Visual Effect:** Distinctive violet photonic flash
