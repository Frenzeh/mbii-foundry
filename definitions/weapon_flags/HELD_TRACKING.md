# Tracking Beacon On Hit

`HELD_TRACKING`

> Applies 45-second wallhack tracking to targets hit by this weapon.

## What it does

When this weapon strikes an enemy, a tracking dart effect is applied for 45 seconds. The attacker and any teammate equipped with `MB_ATT_TRACKING_BEACON` can see the tracked target on HUD/radar through walls.
