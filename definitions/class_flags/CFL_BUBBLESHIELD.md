# Bubble Shield (Shockfield)

`CFL_BUBBLESHIELD`

> Passive Galak-style electrocution and push barrier.

## What it does

Surrounds the player with an electrified energy barrier (similar to Galak in Jedi Outcast). 
- Electrocutes and pushes away enemy players who make contact.
- Remains active as long as the user has Armor Points remaining (can be sustained with armor regeneration).
