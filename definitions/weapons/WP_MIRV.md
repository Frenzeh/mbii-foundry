# MIRV Rocket Launcher

`WP_MIRV`

> Multi-warhead portable rocket system sharing ammo with PLX-1.

## What it does

Fires a cluster volley of multiple explosive rockets per trigger pull. The volley count scales directly with the user's `MB_ATT_MIRV` rank ( + \text{Rank}$).

## Mechanics & Stats

- **Ammo Pool:** Shares ammo with PLX-1 / Rocket Launcher.
- **Volley Count:**  + \text{MB_ATT_MIRV Level}$ rockets per shot.
- **HUD Indicator:** `HUD_PLX1ROCKETS`.
