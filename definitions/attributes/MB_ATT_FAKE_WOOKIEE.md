# Wookalike (Fake Wookiee)

`MB_ATT_FAKE_WOOKIEE`

> Inherits Wookiee animations, melee slap, and fury onto non-Wookiee classes.

## What it does

Allows a character using any `MBClass` to inherit signature Wookiee mechanics:
- Wookiee animation skeleton
- Wookiee Slap secondary melee attack
- Wookiee Fury mechanics

## Related

- `MB_CLASS_WOOKIE`
- `MB_ATT_WOOKIE_STRENGTH`
- `MB_ATT_FURY`
